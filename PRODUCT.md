# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Customers and potential clients visiting the landing page.

## Product Purpose
To educate customers about our product and service, showcasing our capabilities and streamlining their path to engagement.

## Brand Personality
Tech-forward and industrial.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS illustrations and templates.

## Design Principles
- Emphasize mechanical precision and industrial structure.
- Show, don't tell. Let the UI and interactions speak for the product's capability.
- Avoid generic patterns; every design choice should feel intentional and tech-forward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific requirements beyond basic best practices (ensure standard contrast ratios and semantic HTML).
