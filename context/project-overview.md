# Project Overview — TicketFlow B2B Ticketing SaaS Platform

---

## 1. Site Overview

This is a marketing landing page for **TicketFlow**, a B2B SaaS ticketing platform targeting event organizers, marketing teams, ops teams, and enterprise operations. The platform automates the full ticketing lifecycle — from event setup and secure payment collection to QR code delivery and real-time attendee check-in.

The product is positioned as community-first and non-technical: *"Built for people, not programmers."* The tone is warm, direct, and benefit-led — written for busy operators who want a tool that just works.

The landing page is a single long-scroll page structured across ten sections:

- **Hero** — headline, value proposition, and a single primary CTA (Contact Us)
- **Problem** — validates the pain of manual ticketing (spreadsheets, delayed delivery, payment friction, no visibility)
- **How It Works** — a four-step process from event setup to sellout
- **Features** — six core capabilities with a non-technical subheading; designed for community-focused operators
- **Social Proof** — two testimonials plus real stat callouts (10,000+ events, 1M+ tickets, 99.9% uptime)
- **Who It's For** — four audience cards replacing a pricing section (Event Organizers, Marketing Teams, Ops Teams, Enterprise)
- **Integrations** — payment gateways, SMS providers, email tools, CRM
- **FAQ** — five questions covering setup, payments, branding, free trial, and failed delivery
- **Contact** — a structured form with a dropdown for audience type, plus WhatsApp and direct call options
- **Footer** — navigation, legal, newsletter signup, social links, and copyright

---

## 2. Goals

**Primary Goals**
- Drive inbound contact form submissions via the "Contact Us" CTA (the single conversion path in the Hero)
- Qualify visitors by audience type through the Contact form dropdown (Organizer / Marketing / Ops / Enterprise)
- Facilitate direct conversations via WhatsApp and phone call links in the Contact section

**Secondary Goals**
- Build credibility through concrete social proof: 10,000+ events powered, 1M+ tickets delivered, 99.9% uptime
- Eliminate friction by clearly communicating that setup requires no technical expertise
- Differentiate TicketFlow from generic tools by anchoring to community, simplicity, and local events
- Replace pricing anxiety entirely — the "Who It's For" section positions the platform by role, not tier

**Brand Goals**
- Establish TicketFlow as a community-native platform ("Built for community. Ticketing made simple, faster, and local.")
- Make non-technical buyers (event organizers, ops leads, marketing managers) feel immediately at home
- Signal scale without intimidating smaller organizers — from 50-person meetups to 50,000-person festivals

## 3. Core User Flow

The intended path a visitor takes from landing to conversion:

```
1. LAND on Hero Section
   → Read headline ("Sell Tickets. Send QR Codes. Skip the Headaches.")
   → Subheadline confirms it's built for event organizers specifically
   → Single CTA: "Contact Us" — no split decision, one clear next step

2. SCROLL through Problem Section
   → Four pain point bullets mirror the visitor's current reality
   → Closing line reframes the problem as solvable → builds intent to continue

3. UNDERSTAND the product via How It Works
   → Four steps (Set Up → Buy → Deliver → Track) make complexity feel trivial
   → Reduces fear of switching or onboarding effort

4. EVALUATE Features
   → Subheading ("Tools designed for people, not programmers") removes technical anxiety
   → Six features confirm coverage of delivery, payments, analytics, branding, and scale

5. BUILD TRUST via Social Proof
   → Real stat numbers (10,000+ events, 1M+ tickets) signal proven scale
   → Testimonials from peers reinforce peer validation

6. SELF-IDENTIFY via Who It's For
   → Four audience cards let visitors see themselves in the product
   → No pricing pressure — focus is on fit, not cost
   → Enterprise card signals custom support is available without gating it behind a paywall

7. CHECK Integrations
   → Confirms TicketFlow works within their existing stack
   → Reduces "will this fit our tools?" objection

8. RESOLVE DOUBTS via FAQ
   → Removes final objections around setup time, payment support, branding, trial risk

9. CONVERT via Contact Section
   → Fill out form: name, email, company, audience type, optional message
   → Dropdown qualifies lead type for the sales team
   → WhatsApp and Call Us options capture visitors who prefer direct conversation
```
